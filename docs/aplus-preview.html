Frequently Asked Questions

Can the top light and reading light be used separately?
Yes. Each of the three light heads can be switched on and off independently — use just the reading light, just the ambient glow, or any combination that suits the moment.
Does it support dimming and color temperature adjustment?
Yes. The lamp offers 10%–100% stepless dimming and three color temperatures (3000K warm / 4500K natural / 6000K cool), adjustable via the remote, the touch panel, or the app.
Is it controlled by remote?
Yes. The included remote controls power, brightness, and color temperature. You can also control the lamp with the built-in touch panel or the smartphone app — whichever is closest.
